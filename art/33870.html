A pupil of Katsukawa Shunshō, Shun’ei started designing prints around 1778. Although his work is dominated by Kabuki subjects, both as single-sheet prints and as illustrations for playbills ( banzuke ), he is also admired for a series of women performing Kabuki dances and for his sumō-e , or pictures of wrestlers.
