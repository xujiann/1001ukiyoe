属国芳《诚忠义臣名名镜》系列。取材「忠臣藏」——赤穗四十七义士为主君复仇的真实事件，江户时代最受欢迎的题材。幕府禁止直接搬演当代政事，画师遂借古装与化名迂回叙述；国芳以强悍的人物造型与紧张构图，把每位义士单独立传。

From Kuniyoshi's Mirror of Loyal Retainers. The subject is the Chūshingura—the true vendetta of the forty-seven rōnin of Akō, the most popular narrative of the Edo period. Since the shogunate forbade depicting contemporary events, artists used period costume and altered names; Kuniyoshi gave each retainer a separate portrait of forceful build and taut design.
