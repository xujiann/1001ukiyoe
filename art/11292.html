属芳年为《邮便报知新闻》所作的新闻锦绘。明治初年报纸初兴，画师以套色木版把社会奇闻、命案与时事绘成插图随报发行——这是浮世绘向近代新闻插画过渡的关键一步，也是芳年早期最富戏剧性的作品群。

From Yoshitoshi's prints for the Yūbin Hōchi Shimbun. In the early Meiji years, as newspapers took hold, artists rendered sensational news, crimes and current events as colour woodblocks issued with the paper—a pivotal step from ukiyo-e toward modern press illustration, and among Yoshitoshi's most dramatic early work.
