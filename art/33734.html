The Second Month, Matsuchiyama in Snow at Dusk

小林清亲 (Kobayashi Kiyochika)

年代 Date
March 1896

木版画 / Woodblock print

地区 Region
日本 / Japan

Kiyochika produced his first landscape prints-views of Tokyo-in the late 1870s. This triptych shows, however, is characteristics of his later landscape style with its softer tone, lighter colors and obscured outlines. Lacking the western elements frequently found in his earlier prints, these representations of modern Tokyo appeared somewhat more traditional. This snow scene, depicting a ferry boat on the Sumida River
