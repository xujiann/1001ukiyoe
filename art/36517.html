Related to the play Keisei Ōmonguchi けいせい廓大門 (The Great Gate of the Licensed Quarter), performed at the Kado Theater, Osaka, in the 1st month of 1823 (Bunsei 6). This unusual two-tier triptych (a set of three works meant to be shown together) depicts a scene in the play Keisei Ōmonguchi ( The Great Gate of the Licensed Quarter ).
