The Tale of Genji, arguably the most famous Japanese novel, was written by Murasaki Shikibu, an early eleventh century noblewoman. The story centers around Hikaru Genji, the handsome son of an Emperor, and his romantic life. The tale’s implicit eroticism provided fertile ground for explicitly erotic adaptaions.
