春梅斎 北英创作的木版画，约1832年，现藏于克利夫兰艺术博物馆。

This print shows Arashi Rikan II (1788–1837) acting in a Kabuki play put on at the Chikugoza Theater in Osaka in 1832. Rikan’s character, Danshichi Kurōbei, was among the otokodate roles, or men who stood up for those without much power in society. Mirror of Naniwa : The Summer Festival remains popular today and has the most well-known Kabuki murder scene, in which Danshichi kills his deviou
