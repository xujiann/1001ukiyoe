Naniwaya Teahouse Waitress Okita

喜多川歌麿 (Kitagawa Utamaro)

年代 Date
c. 1793
媒材 Medium
木版画 / Woodblock print
馆藏 Collection
明尼阿波利斯艺术学院 / Minneapolis Institute of Art
地区 Region
日本 / Japan

Okita was a beautiful waitress at the Naniwaya tea-house near Asakusa Temple in the city of Edo. When Utamaro created this print of her, she would have been about fifteen years old.
