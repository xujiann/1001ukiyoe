Yamada Hōgyoku

1804–1844 · 日本

49 件作品 / works

山田宝玉，日本江户后期浮世绘画师，活跃于文政至天保年间。存世作品以摺物为主——狂歌连订制的小幅版画，题材多为器物、花卉、动物与店铺招牌一类的应景之作。其生平记载极少。

Yamada Hōgyoku (1804–1844) was an ukiyo-e artist of the late Edo period, active in the Bunsei and Tenpō eras. His surviving work consists largely of surimono — small privately commissioned prints made for kyōka poetry circles — depicting objects, flowers, animals and shop signs. Little is recorded of his life.
