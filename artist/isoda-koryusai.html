矶田湖龙斋（1735年生，卒年不详），日本江户时代中期浮世绘画师，本名矶田正胜，通称庄兵卫。原为土浦藩土屋家武士，后成浪人，居江户两国药研堀。画风深受铃木春信影响，尤擅狭长的柱绘，所作至少三百五十图，数量为同期画师之冠。1776 年起刊行美人画系列《雏形若菜の初模样》，图逾百幅；1782 年获法桥僧位，晚年转以肉笔浮世绘为主。

Isoda Koryūsai (born 1735; date of death unknown) was a Japanese ukiyo-e artist of the mid-Edo period. A samurai in the service of the Tsuchiya clan of Tsuchiura, he became a rōnin and lived in the Yagenbori district of Edo. Working in a manner close to Suzuki Harunobu's, he specialised in narrow pillar prints, designing at least 350 of them — more than any other artist of his generation. From 1776 he issued the long beauty series Hinagata Wakana no Hatsu Moyō, received the Buddhist rank of hokkyō in 1782, and in his later years worked mainly as a painter.
