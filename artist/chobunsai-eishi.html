鸟文斋荣之

Chōbunsai Eishi

1756–1829 · 日本

64 件作品 / works

鸟文斋荣之，出身武士的浮世绘大师。他笔下的美人身姿修长、气质高华，透着贵族式的优雅娴静，自成一格。

Chōbunsai Eishi was a samurai-born ukiyo-e master. His beauties are slender and aristocratic, radiating a refined, courtly grace all his own.
