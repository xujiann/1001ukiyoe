落合芳几

Utagawa Yoshiiku

1833–1904 · 日本

18 件作品 / works

落合芳几，歌川国芳门下，幕末明治的浮世绘师。他以役者绘著称，曾与芳年合作骇人的「无惨绘」，明治维新后转向新闻插画，是浮世绘走向近代报刊的过渡人物。

Utagawa Yoshiiku, a pupil of Kuniyoshi, was an ukiyo-e artist of the late Edo and Meiji eras. Known for actor prints, he collaborated with Yoshitoshi on the gruesome 'bloody prints' (muzan-e) and later turned to newspaper illustration—a bridge from ukiyo-e to the modern Meiji press.
