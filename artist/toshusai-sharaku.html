东洲斋写乐

Tōshūsai Sharaku

?–1801 · 日本

64 件作品 / works

东洲斋写乐，浮世绘史上最神秘的大师。他仅在 1794–95 年活跃约十个月，创作约 140 幅极其大胆、直指人物内心的役者绘，随即销声匿迹，真实身份至今成谜。

Tōshūsai Sharaku is the most mysterious master of ukiyo-e. Active for only about ten months in 1794–95, he produced some 140 startlingly candid, psychologically penetrating actor portraits and then vanished; his true identity is still debated.
