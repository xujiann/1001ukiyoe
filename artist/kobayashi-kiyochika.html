小林清亲

Kobayashi Kiyochika

1847–1915 · 日本

131 件作品 / works

小林清亲，人称「最后的浮世绘大师」。他开创「光线画」，以西洋式的光影明暗描绘明治东京的煤气灯、铁道与暮色，为古老的木版画注入近代都市的诗意；亦作战争题材版画。

Kobayashi Kiyochika is often called the last great master of ukiyo-e. He pioneered kōsen-ga ('light-ray pictures'), using Western-influenced light and shadow to capture the gaslight, railways and twilight of Meiji Tokyo, bringing a modern urban poetry to the old woodblock medium; he also made war prints.
