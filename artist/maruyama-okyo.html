円山応挙

Maruyama Ōkyo

1733–1795 · 日本

27 件作品 / works

円山応挙，日本江户中期京都画家，圆山派开创者。生于丹波国桑田郡穴太村（今京都府龟冈市），少年赴京都，入狩野派系统画师石田幽汀门下。二十多岁时在玩具商尾张屋绘制过借窥镜观看的「眼镜绘」，由此接触西洋透视法。他随身带写生帖，多角度对照实物描摹动植物。代表作《雪松图屏风》为国宝，藏三井纪念美术馆。门人有长泽芦雪等；松村月溪（呉春）本师从与谢芜村，曾求入其门，応挙不受而以友相待，月溪后另开四条派。

Maruyama Ōkyo (1733–1795) was a Kyoto painter of the mid-Edo period and founder of the Maruyama school. Born in Ano-o village in Tanba Province, he trained as a boy under the Kanō-lineage painter Ishida Yūtei; in his twenties, working for a Kyoto toy dealer, he made megane-e, peep-box pictures that introduced him to Western perspective. He carried sketchbooks everywhere, drawing plants and animals from direct observation. His Pine Trees in Snow screens are designated a National Treasure and are held by the Mitsui Memorial Museum, Tokyo. Nagasawa Rosetsu studied under him; Matsumura Goshun, a pupil of Yosa Buson, asked to enter his studio but Ōkyo declined and treated him as a friend and equal, and Goshun later founded the Shijō school.
