神坂雪佳

Kamisaka Sekka

1866–1942 · 日本

63 件作品 / works

神坂雪佳，日本近代京都画家、图案家，被视为琳派最后一位重要传人。生于京都粟田口，早年习四条派绘画，后随岸光景研习工艺图案而转向琳派。1901年受派赴欧洲考察图案，正值新艺术运动兴起。他为染织、陶艺、漆艺设计纹样，1909年起由芸艸堂刊行木版图案集《百々世草》三卷。

Kamisaka Sekka (1866–1942), born in Kyoto, was a painter and design artist, and the last major figure of the Rinpa tradition. Trained first in Shijō-school painting, he turned to Rinpa through his craft-design teacher Kishi Kōkei. Sent to Europe in 1901 to study design, he encountered Art Nouveau. He designed patterns for textiles, ceramics and lacquer, and published the three-volume woodblock album Momoyogusa from 1909.
