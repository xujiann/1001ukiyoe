溪斋英泉

Keisai Eisen

1791–1848 · 日本

28 件作品 / works

溪斋英泉，以妖冶的美人画和风景版画著称的浮世绘师。他笔下的女性风情浓艳、别具颓废之美，并与广重合作了《木曾街道六十九次》。

Keisai Eisen was an ukiyo-e master known for sensuous bijin-ga and landscape prints. His women have a heavy, decadent allure, and he collaborated with Hiroshige on the Sixty-nine Stations of the Kiso Road.
