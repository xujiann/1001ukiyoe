谷文晁

Tani Bunchō

1763–1841 · 日本

27 件作品 / works

谷文晁（1763–1841），日本江户后期画家。早年学狩野派，后转向南画，兼取汉、和、洋诸法，画风有「八宗兼学」之称。曾为白河藩主松平定信近习，1793年随其巡视相模、伊豆沿海海防，作《公余探胜图》。在江户设画塾写山楼，渡边华山、立原杏所等出其门下。

Tani Bunchō (1763–1841) was a Japanese painter of the late Edo period. Trained first in the Kanō school and then in the nanga manner, he freely combined Chinese, Japanese and Western techniques, a range often summed up as "a student of all eight sects". He served Matsudaira Sadanobu, lord of Shirakawa, accompanying his 1793 coastal-defence inspection of the Sagami and Izu shores to produce the Kōyo tanshō zu. His Edo studio Shazanrō taught Watanabe Kazan and Tachihara Kyōsho.
