菊川英山

Kikukawa Eizan

1787–1867 · 日本

44 件作品 / works

菊川英山（1787–1867），日本江户后期浮世绘画师，以美人画著称。父菊川英二在江户市谷经营造花业「近江屋」，兼习狩野派画艺（狩野东舍门人）；英山先随父学画，后师从圆山四条派的铃木南岭（1775–1844）。文化三年（1806）歌麿猝逝后，他接续歌麿风的美人样式，成为文化年间美人绘的第一人；进入文政年间，时尚转向其门人溪斋英泉与歌川国贞的艳丽新样，英山声势渐衰。溪斋英泉出其门下，他本人则被视为菊川派之祖。文政末年（约1830）版画工作渐止，晚年专事肉笔画。

Kikukawa Eizan (1787–1867) was an ukiyo-e designer best known for bijin-ga, images of beauties. He first studied with his father Eiji, who ran an artificial-flower business in Edo and painted in the Kanō manner, and then with Suzuki Nanrei (1775–1844) of the Maruyama-Shijō school. After Utamaro's sudden death in 1806 Eizan took up his manner of depicting women and became the foremost bijin designer of the Bunka era (1804–18); in the Bunsei era taste moved on to the more sensuous beauties of his own pupil Keisai Eisen and of Utagawa Kunisada, and his popularity faded. He is regarded as the founder of the Kikukawa school. His print work tailed off around the end of Bunsei (c. 1830), and in later years he worked mainly as a painter.
