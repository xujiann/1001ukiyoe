鸟居清长

Torii Kiyonaga

1752–1815 · 日本

138 件作品 / works

鸟居清长，鸟居派宗师，天明年间美人画的定义者。他笔下修长挺拔、雍容端丽的全身美人，构图开阔、气度高华，将浮世绘美人画推向黄金时代的一个高峰。

Torii Kiyonaga, head of the Torii school, defined the bijin-ga of the 1780s. His tall, stately full-length beauties, set in spacious compositions of great elegance, brought the ukiyo-e depiction of women to one of its golden-age peaks.
